01 Introduction
Imaxtel Intl General Trading Co. W.L.L is a 
Kuwait-based general trading company 
specializing in the import and distribution of 
high-quality Fast-Moving Consumer Goods 
(FMCG). Since its establishment in 2010, the 
company has built a strong reputation for 
reliability, quality products, and efficient supply 
chain management.
With over a decade of experience in the 
industry, Imaxtel sources products from global 
markets including UAE, Philippines, Thailand, 
Vietnam, Singapore, India and Poland 
ensuring a diverse and premium product 
portfolio for customers across Kuwait.  
Our commitment is to deliver quality products, dependable service, and 
strong partnerships, supporting businesses and communities with 
products that meet international standards and customer expectations
Delivering trusted FMCG products through global sourcing and reliable distribution

Our Key Divisions
Imaxtel Intl General Trading Co. W.L.L serves as the parent company 
overseeing a group of businesses operating in the FMCG sector. 
Through its divisions and subsidiaries, Imaxtel manages product 
sourcing, import operations, distribution, and retail activities across 
Kuwait
Our  Key  Divisions-
Malabar Food Stuff is a key wholesale division of Imaxtel Intl General 
Trading Co. W.L.L, serving as an important hub for the distribution of 
grocery and FMCG products in Kuwait. Located in the Shuwaikh 
Industrial Area, the store focuses on supplying a wide range of food and 
grocery items to retailers, restaurants, supermarkets, and catering 
businesses.
To support efficient logistics and timely product delivery, Malabar Food 
Stuff operates a dedicated fleet of delivery vehicles. The division currently 
maintains 5+ delivery vehicles, including vans and half lorry, which 
enable smooth transportation of goods across different locations in 
Kuwait
This fleet plays a vital role in ensuring fast, reliable, and organized 
distribution, allowing the company to handle bulk orders, maintain 
consistent supply to retailers and businesses, and meet customer 
demands with efficiency.
